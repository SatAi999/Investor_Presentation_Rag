================================================================================
  FAILURE ANALYSIS & KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

  1. Table/chart text flattening (impacts Q1, Q5)
     pdfplumber extracts table data as prose. Numerical figures that appear
     in bar charts are not extracted at all (they are rendered as images in
     the source PDF). The revenue figures were readable from text-based tables
     on pages 4 and 6, but the faithfulness judge could not match the extracted
     prose to the original chart format, yielding FF=0.00 on Q1 in baseline.

  2. Strategy question retrieves ESG slides (impacts Q3)
     The word "initiatives" semantically overlaps with sustainability/ESG content.
     The retriever surfaced pages 17–18 (sustainability) instead of pages 22–28
     (growth strategy). A query expansion or re-ranking step would mitigate this.

  3. Risk disclosure in legal boilerplate (impacts Q4)
     The forward-looking statement disclaimer on page 2 contains the risk
     language, but it is written in dense legal prose. The 7B llama2 model failed
     to extract it as a direct answer to a plain-English risk question.

  4. Single shared collection
     All ingested PDFs share one Qdrant collection. Chunks from the Assignment.pdf
     (5 pages, ingested for testing) are mixed with Trent Q2FY26 (35 pages).
     This can introduce irrelevant citations. In production, a per-company
     collection or a metadata filter on `company` field should be used.
